Resolve gene/splicing compatibility through UMI counting from CSTRAD2

Usage: constellation count-splicing [OPTIONS] --assignments <ASSIGNMENTS> --out-prefix <OUT_PREFIX>

Options:
      --assignments <ASSIGNMENTS>
          CSTRAD2 produced by map --splicing-state (plain, gzip, or zstd)
      --barcode-whitelist <BARCODE_WHITELIST>
          
      --no-correct-barcodes
          
      --no-correct-umis
          
      --resolution <RESOLUTION>
          Molecule resolver; usa applies alevin-fry cr-like/WTA spliced tie preference. Currently requires --no-correct-umis [default: intersection] [possible values: intersection, usa]
      --emit-metrics <EMIT_METRICS>
          
      --out-prefix <OUT_PREFIX>
          
  -h, --help
          Print help
